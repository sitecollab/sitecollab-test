Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What if I need more pages later than my package covers?

We quote any extra pages as a small separate job — no need to move up a whole tier just to add one page.

I don't know anything technical — is that a problem?

No. We build the site with you and hand it over ready to use. Aftercare exists for exactly this reason, if you'd rather not touch it yourself at all.

Who pays for hosting, and how much is it?

Hosting runs through your own IONOS account, billed to you directly at IONOS's rates. We don't add a markup or bill you for it separately.

What if I need more than two Aftercare requests in a month?

Just ask — anything beyond the usual scope is quoted upfront before we start, so there's never a surprise bill.

Is there a contract?

No. The website build is a one-off fixed fee. Aftercare, if you choose it, is billed monthly and you can stop whenever you like.
